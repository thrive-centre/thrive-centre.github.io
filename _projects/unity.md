## About UNITY

UNITY is a UK research consortium bringing together clinical and academic partners to apply AI to improve how heart scans are performed and interpreted. It combines expertise in echocardiography, medical imaging and artificial intelligence across universities, professional bodies and NHS hospitals.

## Aims

- **Standardise** echocardiography through AI-based tools.
- **Accelerate** image acquisition, analysis and annotation.
- **Democratise** access by making tools and resources open across clinical and global settings.

## Part of AI in Echocardiography

UNITY is the flagship research programme within [AI in Echocardiography](/projects/ai-in-echocardiography/). It works alongside the [Echocardiography AI Ecosystem](/projects/ai-in-echocardiography/#ecosystem), a growing set of resources and applications for the whole theme: [Biobank](/datasets/) | [EchoForge](/projects/echoforge/) | [EchoLearn](/projects/echolearn/) | [EchoCruise](/projects/echocruise/).
